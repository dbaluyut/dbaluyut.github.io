From exhibit spaces to software interfaces

What my design background brings to engineering.

By Derrique Baluyut · October 28, 2026

From the work

Production engineering, design, and the practice around the code.

A different kind of user journey

Before software engineering, I worked in trade show design and technical drafting. At Freeman, I considered attendee traffic, graphic opportunities, client goals, and production constraints. Those were practical questions about how people move through an experience.

Make the next step clear

Software has different tools and constraints, but clarity still matters. A seller checking an account status or an operator reviewing a workflow needs to understand the context and available actions. My design background is a useful perspective when I collaborate with UX and engineering teams.

Work has to reach production

Exhibit design also taught me to coordinate with vendors and internal departments to carry a concept into production. In engineering, I have worked on testing, controlled releases, on-call support, and shared library documentation. In both settings, the work continues beyond the initial design.
