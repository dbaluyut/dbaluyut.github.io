01 / Featured work

Two disciplines. One approach.

Where exhibit design and software engineering come together · Live MVP

Exhibit Graphics Studio

This project brings my two careers together: firsthand experience with exhibit graphics and production workflows, paired with the engineering skills to build a working browser-based solution.

Problem I experienced as a designer: Clients frequently requested new renderings of exhibit structures and entrance units whenever artwork changed. Even simple revisions took time away from other design work.

Solution I built as an engineer: A working 3D viewer where clients can explore an exhibit, download panel templates, and replace artwork on predefined surfaces in real time — without waiting for a designer to re-render the scene.

Future direction: Extend the viewer into a production artwork workflow, where clients submit final print-ready files and route them to the appropriate departments for review and approval.
