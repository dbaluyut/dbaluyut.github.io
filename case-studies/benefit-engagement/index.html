Case study 04 / Benefit Engagement

Delivering personalized CMS content

Orchestration-layer contributions for targeted frontend content maintained in a proprietary CMS.

The context

Content maintained in a CMS still has to reach the right frontend experience through predictable interfaces. Benefit Engagement focused on delivering personalized content to targeted users.

CMS content → Queries + templates → Targeted experience

Conceptual view of the work, not a production architecture diagram.

My role
Contributed GraphQL integrations, CMS template seeds, service documentation, and observability.

What I worked on

  • Designed GraphQL queries and maintained schemas and high-level designs and orchestration-layer contracts in Jira.
  • Created CMS template seeds and verified them with the frontend team.
  • Used Splunk to explore HTTP logs and created alerts and dashboards for feature work; debugged queries with Postman.
  • Participated in proprietary-tool A/B testing, served as Scrum master on assigned sprints, and used Looper and Concord for CI/CD.
GraphQLCMSSplunk

Engineering perspective

Content delivery includes the integration contract and the ability to see what happens in production. Templates, query behavior, and observability were connected parts of my contribution.

What I delivered

CMS integration and GraphQL work for personalized content delivery, supported by feature-specific alerts and dashboards.

Targeting logic, proprietary templates, and internal dashboards are not reproduced. The summary describes my contributions within a wider team; it does not claim sole ownership of the overall product or quantified business impact.
