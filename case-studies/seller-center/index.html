Case study 01 / Seller Center Enforcement

Making account status actionable

A seller-facing microfrontend for account status, suspension, appeals, and GMV Pause notifications.

The context

Sellers need to understand their account status and the actions available to them. Enforcement experiences bring together status information, suspension details, appeal flows, and notifications in one part of Seller Center.

Account status → Available actions → Appeal / notification

Conceptual view of the work, not a production architecture diagram.

My role
Built and owned the enforcement microfrontend from initial release; served as codeowner.

What I worked on

  • Built account-status widgets, suspension and appeal flows, and GMV Pause notifications.
  • Implemented multilingual and market-specific content, including zh-CN support.
  • Supported feature-flag-controlled rollouts and served as codeowner for the microfrontend.
ReactTypeScriptMicrofrontends

Engineering perspective

Status is only useful when a person can understand what it means and what to do next. This work connected account information to concrete seller-facing workflows while supporting differences across markets.

What I delivered

A seller-facing enforcement microfrontend carried from initial release into ongoing ownership.

The production interface and internal implementation are not reproduced here. The summary describes my contributions within a wider team; it does not claim sole ownership of the overall product or quantified business impact.
