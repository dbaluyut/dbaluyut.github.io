Case study 03 / Pet Pharmacy

Connecting prescription commerce

Query and orchestration work for pet medication ordering that requires veterinarian approval.

EngagementWalmart via Randstad · 2022–2026
FocusGraphQL & integration
In this study01 / Context02 / My contribution03 / Engineering perspective04 / Delivery

The context

Prescription pet medication ordering has an approval requirement beyond a typical retail purchase. The e-commerce experience needs the appropriate data and service contracts to support that journey.

Shopping experience → GraphQL contracts → Service integration

Conceptual view of the work, not a production architecture diagram.

My role
Contributed GraphQL queries, schemas, orchestration-layer work, testing, and production support.

What I worked on

  • Designed and implemented GraphQL queries, maintained schemas, and built Node.js orchestration resolvers.
  • Maintained high-level designs and orchestration-layer contracts in Confluence; debugged queries with Postman and HTTP logs with Splunk.
  • Wrote and maintained Jest unit tests, participated in E2E and integration testing, and documented feature testing.
  • Participated in A/B testing and company-wide holiday on-call support; used Looper and Concord for CI/CD and served as Scrum master on assigned sprints.
GraphQLNode.jsJest

Engineering perspective

A query is part of a larger contract between the interface and its services. Maintaining designs and contracts alongside tests and debugging tools helped me work across those boundaries.

What I delivered

GraphQL and orchestration contributions supporting the prescription commerce experience, with maintained contracts and feature tests.

This is an engineering contribution summary, not a disclosure of prescription data, approval rules, or service endpoints. The summary describes my contributions within a wider team; it does not claim sole ownership of the overall product or quantified business impact.
